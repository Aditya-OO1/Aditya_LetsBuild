JURA: justice in days, not years.

My own product: an AI-powered online dispute resolution platform for India. Describe a problem in plain words, get free AI guidance, then resolve it through online mediation or arbitration with verified lawyers, all in one place.

Acknowledged by Prof. (Dr.) Anil SahasrabudheFormer Chairman of AICTE and Chairman of NAAC
Supreme Court of IndiaAcknowledgement for JURA
The problem

5 crore+ cases are pending in Indian courts.

A failed UPI payment, a bounced cheque or an unpaid salary can take years to resolve. People don’t know their options or the likely outcome, and lawyers, paperwork and court visits cost too much for a small dispute, so many simply give up.

Source: National Judicial Data Grid, May 2026

How a case moves

  1. 01Tell us what happened

    In plain words, no legal jargon.

  2. 02Get instant AI guidance

    Similar past cases and a suggested path.

  3. 03Bring in a lawyer

    A verified lawyer reviews the evidence and represents you.

  4. 04Get resolved

    A mediated agreement or a binding arbitration award.

Built for three kinds of users

Citizens

Free help first, then a real resolution

  • Free AI legal guidance, matched against 90+ dispute patterns across banking, fintech, property, family, employment, consumer and corporate cases
  • Structured answers: the situation, similar cases, their options and a next step
  • Verified lawyers to browse by specialty, experience and fee
  • Mediation or binding arbitration, 100% online
  • Document scanner that reads IDs and evidence and flags errors, like an Aadhaar number of the wrong length
  • Chat, payments and case tracking
Lawyers & arbitrators

A second opinion before every decision

  • Case inbox and hearings with private notes and direct client chat
  • The AI Case Assistant reviews a draft decision for reasoning, appeal risk and missed arguments
  • It recommends go ahead, adjust or rethink, and the arbitrator makes the final call
  • A downloadable Case Summary PDF of issues, arguments, conclusion and AI feedback
Admin

Full control of the platform

  • Triage incoming cases
  • Assign verified lawyers
  • Manage people and roles
  • Handle deletion requests
  • Oversee every case

How the AI works

Citizen AI

Matches the dispute to past patterns in a precedent library first, then gives that context to Gemini 2.5 Flash, so answers are specific, not generic.

Arbitrator AI

Reviews draft decisions using the model’s legal reasoning and knowledge.

Document AI

Extracts and validates fields from uploaded documents.

Next: a Legal AI of our own

Turning the precedent library into a legal ontology of Indian dispute law, then training a dedicated model: answers traceable to statute and precedent, regional languages and a lower cost per query.

Why it’s different

CourtsOften yearsNo first-step guidance
Lawyer onlyWeeks to monthsPaid consultation, unpredictable cost
JURA25–30 day targetFree AI check, fixed fee, 100% online
Business model

A fixed filing fee by claim size, from ₹5,000 to ₹4,00,000. Businesses and fintechs pay 1% of the claim value. The free AI check brings users in, and revenue comes when they file a case or book a lawyer.

Starting market

High-volume, fully digital consumer and fintech disputes: UPI debited but not credited, delayed refunds, loan-app harassment, BNPL and credit-score errors, and unauthorised debits.
